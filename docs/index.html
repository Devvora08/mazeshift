Maze Shift Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Maze Shift, developed by Dev Vora, handles information when you use the game.

Information stored on your device

Maze Shift stores campaign progress, level records, active-run information, purchase-access status and game settings locally on your device. This information is used to provide game functionality and is not uploaded to a Maze Shift account. Maze Shift does not require you to create an account.

You can remove locally stored information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Doing so may erase campaign progress and settings. A qualifying Google Play purchase can be restored separately through the game.

Purchases

Maze Shift offers an optional one-time in-app purchase through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment. The developer does not receive or store your complete payment-card or bank-account details.

RevenueCat is used to validate purchases, provide purchased access and support purchase restoration. In doing so, Google Play and RevenueCat may process purchase history, transaction information, an anonymous app user identifier, device or platform information, country information and technical information needed to operate and protect the purchasing service.

How information is used

Purchase-related information is used to:

  • Confirm and restore the full-game purchase.
  • Provide access to purchased levels.
  • Prevent purchase errors and fraud.
  • Provide purchase support and understand purchase-service performance.

Advertising and third-party sales

Maze Shift does not contain advertising. The developer does not sell your personal information.

Data sharing and service providers

Information needed to process and validate purchases may be handled by Google Play and RevenueCat as service providers. Their handling of information is also governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is cleared or the app is removed. Purchase records may be retained by Google and RevenueCat as required to provide purchase restoration, maintain financial records, prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.

For help identifying or requesting deletion of a RevenueCat customer record, contact the developer and include the Purchase support ID shown in the game's Settings section. Deleting a RevenueCat record does not delete records that Google must retain and does not cancel or refund a Google Play purchase.

Children's privacy

Maze Shift is not specifically directed to young children and does not knowingly request personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the game, contact the developer.

Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information processed in connection with the game. No electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game, its service providers or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are published.
